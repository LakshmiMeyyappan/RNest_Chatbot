Category: About RNest
Q: What is RNest?
A: RNest (RNest Healthcare Pvt. Ltd.) is a home healthcare coordination service for ageing parents in India. It gives families one team, one number and one plan for every healthcare need: nurse visits, doctor consultations, lab tests, medicines, physiotherapy, hospital help, insurance claims and 24/7 emergency response. A dedicated Care Manager coordinates everything.

---
Category: About RNest
Q: Who is RNest designed for?
A: RNest is designed for families who want professional healthcare at home for their parents in India. It is especially useful for people who live far away, for example in Toronto or Boston, while their parents are in Chennai. It also helps families whose parents are recovering from a procedure or have ongoing care needs.

Category: Services
Q: What does the 24/7 Emergency and Ambulance service include?
A: It includes ambulance dispatch (BLS and ALS), coordination with the hospital network, ER hand-off with a Care Manager present, and real-time updates to the family. One call to the 24/7 emergency desk dispatches an ambulance, prepares the hospital and keeps the family updated.

---
Category: Services
Q: What kind of doctor consultations are available?
A: Verified doctors are available through home visits (for GPs and specialists), clinic appointments or teleconsultations. Teleconsults come with a transcript and prescription. RNest also coordinates specialist referrals and chronic-disease management plans.

---
Category: Services
Q: What does nursing and attendant care cover?
A: Trained nurses handle clinical tasks such as vitals monitoring, injections, IVs, wound care and catheter care, and support for post-operative and chronic conditions. Attendants help with daily living such as bathing, mobility and meals. Staff are matched to the parent's condition.

---
Category: Services
Q: Does RNest offer physiotherapy at home?
A: Yes. Services include personalised rehab plans, post-stroke and post-surgery recovery, chronic pain and arthritis management, and fall prevention and balance training. Progress is tracked.

---
Category: Services
Q: How do lab tests and diagnostics work?
A: Blood and urine samples are collected at home. RNest also coordinates imaging such as X-ray and ECG. Reports are digital, stored in the RNest app and shared with the doctor, and trends and alerts are tracked for chronic conditions.

---
Category: Services
Q: How does RNest help with medicines?
A: RNest provides smart medication reminders, adherence tracking and alerts, auto-refills before stock runs out, and doorstep delivery. The app shows which doses were taken and which are due.

---
Category: Services
Q: Can RNest help with hospital admission and discharge?
A: Yes. RNest helps with hospital selection and pre-admission, cashless TPA paperwork, family liaison inside the hospital, and a discharge and recovery-at-home plan.

---
Category: Services
Q: What is hospital visit assistance?
A: RNest accompanies your parent to clinic and hospital visits. This includes pickup and drop-off, in-clinic support, doctor coordination and note-taking, collecting prescriptions and reports, and a family debrief after every visit.

---
Category: Services
Q: Does RNest help with insurance claims?
A: Yes. RNest handles cashless pre-authorisation, claim documents and submission, follow-up with the TPA or insurer, and reimbursement tracking until settlement.

Category: Care Manager
Q: What does a Care Manager do?
A: A Care Manager is one named person who knows your family. They are your single point of contact and coordinate nurses, doctors, labs and pharmacy. They send weekly summaries and instant alerts, and they are available across your time zone.

A: RNest has three main packages: Essential Care at Rs 1,500 per month, Plus Care at Rs 3,000 per month (marked as most popular) and Elite Care at Rs 8,000 per month. A custom plan is also possible through the Contact page.

Q: What is included in the Essential Care package?
A: Essential Care (Rs 1,500 per month) includes 24/7 emergency response, one doctor teleconsultation per month, an annual diagnostics package, real-time family updates in the app, expert talks on nutrition and wellness, and access to discounted add-on services. It is best for parents who are generally stable but want emergency cover and monthly doctor guidance.

Category: Getting started
Q: How does RNest work?
A: There are four steps. First, tell RNest about your parent's condition, concerns and routine by phone, WhatsApp or the form. Second, a Care Manager assesses the needs and suggests a right-sized plan. Third, vetted and trained professionals are matched to your parent. Fourth, you stay informed with daily notes, weekly summaries and instant alerts.

Category: Response and emergencies
Q: What should I do in a medical emergency?
A: Call the RNest 24/7 emergency desk or press the call button in the RNest app. RNest dispatches an ambulance, prepares the hospital and updates the family in real time. If the situation is life-threatening, also call your local emergency number right away.
